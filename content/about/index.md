My name is Christopher Thompson (GitHub: ChrisLeeThompson).

I have been working with scanning electron and focused ion beam microscopes (SEM and FIB) for approximately 15 years. The majority of microscopes I have used are from FEI and Thermo Fisher Scientific (TFS). The scripts on this page are designed and built from that experience, and many interactions with wonderful electron microscopists!

If you have any questions or comments about this page or the scripts, please let me know on [GitHub](https://github.com/ChrisLeeThompson).

Thank you!
